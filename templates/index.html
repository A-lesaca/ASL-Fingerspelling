Practice

Spell a target word and get scored on it — how many letters you got first time, how long each took, and which ones came out wrong.

Teach a letter

Click a letter (or press its key), then get your hand into the sign — you have three seconds before it starts recording.

Whole-word gestures

Teach a sign that types a whole word. It trains exactly like a letter, so it has to be a still pose — signs defined by movement won't work.
